THE BLACK VEIL
==============

An action-adventure. A night in the village of Black Hollow, a blade, a gun
with few rounds, and something under the mill.

To play, double-click "The Black Veil.exe". Keep every file in this folder together.

CONTROLS

  W A S D, mouse       Move, turn the camera
  Left mouse or J      Ashen Blade (sword)
  Right mouse or Q     Hold to target the nearest enemy
  F                    Mourning Star (gun): rounds are few, so count them
  Space                Roll
  E                    Talk, open doors
  Esc                  Quit

NEEDS

  Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics driver with OpenGL 3.3.
  If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  If the game closes at once, "The Black Veil.log" in this folder says why.

Made with moonBASIC.
