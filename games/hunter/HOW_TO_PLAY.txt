GREENWOOD
=========

A hunting game. Hunt the forest island with your bow: four hares, three deer
and two boar. When the hunt is done, the white stag comes out on the high ground.

To play, double-click Greenwood.exe. Keep every file in this folder together.

CONTROLS

  W A S D, mouse       Move, turn the camera
  Shift                Run (animals hear you from farther off)
  C or Left Ctrl       Sneak: slow and quiet
  Space                Roll
  Right mouse or Q     Hold to lock on to the nearest animal ahead
  Left mouse or F      Hold to draw the bow, let go to shoot
  E                    Take what you hunted
  Esc                  Quit

TIPS

  - A full draw flies faster and hits twice as hard.
  - Locked on, your arrow leads a running animal for you.
  - Deer bolt when they hear you, and the herd goes with them. Sneak up.
  - Boar don't run. They charge. Roll out of the way.
  - Missed arrows stick in the ground and trees: walk over them to pick them up.
  - Sit by the campfire to heal.

NEEDS

  Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics driver with OpenGL 3.3.
  If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  If the game closes at once, Greenwood.log in this folder says why.
